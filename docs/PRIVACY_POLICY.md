# Privacy Policy — Social Kids

**Last updated:** September 24, 2026

Social Kids ("the app") is a local-first media player designed for children and managed by their parents. This policy explains what information the app handles and what it does not.

## Information the App Does Not Collect

The app does **not**:

- Collect, transmit, or store personal information on any server
- Include advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs
- Create user accounts or require sign-in
- Share data with third parties
- Use cookies or tracking technologies
- Collect location, contacts, device identifiers, or usage analytics

## Information Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored **locally on your device only**:

| Data | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Media library (titles, URLs, shelves) | Display and organize curated content |
| Watch history and progress | Show parents what was watched |
| Parent PIN / settings | Parental controls and time limits |
| Cached video files (max 500 MB) | Faster playback of previously watched videos |
| Camera photos/videos (if recorded) | Saved to your device gallery |

This data never leaves your device unless **you** explicitly export it.

## Optional Backup (Manual)

You may choose to export your media library as a CSV file and save it to a file or share it yourself. This is entirely voluntary and under your control. The app does not upload this file automatically.

## Permissions

- **Camera** — only when you choose to record a video or take a photo within the app
- **Photos/Media** — to read local video files you select and save recordings
- **Internet** — to stream YouTube embeds and online video content
- **Screen Pinning** — to keep the child screen locked during use

## Children's Privacy

The app is designed for family use. No personal data is collected from any user, including children. Parental controls are managed locally by the parent.

## Changes

If this policy changes, an updated version will be included with the app update.
