# Social Kids — Full Description

## Version 1

Give your child a video experience you can trust. Social Kids is a parent-curated media player built for families — no ads, no algorithms, no endless rabbit holes.

**How it works:**

You're in control. As a parent, you add the videos — YouTube links, direct video files, or content from your device. Organize them into shelves like "Cartoons," "Learning," or "Bedtime." Your child opens the app and sees only what you've approved.

**Built for parents, made for kids:**

- **Parent Mode** — Manage content, shelves, and settings behind a PIN lock
- **Curated Shelves** — Organize videos into categories your child can browse
- **Watch History** — See what your child watched and for how long
- **Time Limits** — Set daily screen time limits and bedtime windows
- **Shorts Feed** — Vertical short-form player for quick, supervised clips
- **Local Media** — Play videos stored directly on your device
- **Photo & Video** — Record or capture moments within the app

**Privacy-first by design:**

- No ads, ever
- No tracking or analytics
- No account required
- No data leaves your device unless you export it
- Works 100% offline for local media

**Safe video playback:**

YouTube content plays through the official embed player only during active viewing. No recommendations, no comments, no related videos — just the content you chose.

**Simple backup:**

Export your entire media library as a CSV file and restore it anytime. Your curation work is never lost.

Social Kids puts parents back in charge. You choose the content, you set the rules, your child enjoys a safe, distraction-free video experience.
